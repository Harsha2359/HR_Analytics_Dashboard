# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📝 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze employee data and provide meaningful insights into workforce performance, employee demographics, experience, salary levels, departments, job roles, and employee attrition.

The dashboard helps HR teams understand **why employees leave the organization**, identify departments and job roles with higher attrition, and analyze employee characteristics such as age, gender, salary slab, and experience.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall employee workforce statistics.
- Monitor employee attrition and attrition rate.
- Identify departments with higher employee attrition.
- Analyze attrition across different salary slabs.
- Understand attrition based on job roles and job satisfaction.
- Study employee distribution across different age groups.
- Analyze attrition by gender.
- Understand the relationship between employee experience and attrition.
- Provide HR teams with an interactive dashboard for better decision-making.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures, calculations, and KPIs
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Data source and preprocessing

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Employee Overview

Provides a quick summary of the organization's workforce through KPI cards:

- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Experience

### 2. Attrition by Department

A donut chart is used to analyze employee attrition across different departments:

- Administration
- Sales
- Operations
- IT
- Marketing
- Human Resources
- Finance

This helps identify departments that contribute significantly to overall employee attrition.

### 3. Attrition by Salary Slab

The dashboard compares employees who stayed and employees who left across different salary ranges:

- 0–3 LPA
- 3–6 LPA
- 6–10 LPA
- 10+ LPA

This provides insights into whether salary levels have an impact on employee attrition.

### 4. Attrition by Job Role & Job Satisfaction

A matrix visual analyzes attrition based on:

- Job Role
- Job Satisfaction Level
- Total Employees Leaving

This helps HR identify job roles and satisfaction levels associated with higher attrition.

### 5. Age Group Distribution

Employee distribution is analyzed across different age groups:

- 18–25
- 26–35
- 36–45
- 46–55
- 55+

This provides an understanding of the organization's workforce demographics.

### 6. Attrition by Gender

A pie chart shows employee attrition based on gender and helps identify differences in attrition patterns.

### 7. Attrition Trend by Experience

The dashboard analyzes attrition based on **total years of experience**, helping identify experience levels where employee turnover is higher.

### 8. Department-wise Employee Count

A funnel-style visualization compares employee counts across departments and provides an overview of workforce distribution.

### 9. Age Group Filter

An interactive age-group slicer allows users to filter the entire dashboard based on:

- 18–25
- 26–35
- 36–45
- 46–55
- 55+

## 🔍 Key Insights

Some important insights that can be derived from the dashboard include:

- The organization has **1,417 employees**, with **231 employees having left**, resulting in an attrition rate of approximately **16.3%**.
- The dashboard enables HR teams to identify departments with comparatively higher attrition.
- Lower salary slabs can be compared with higher salary slabs to understand possible salary-related attrition patterns.
- Employee attrition can be examined across different job satisfaction levels.
- Age-group analysis helps identify the employee groups contributing more to workforce turnover.
- Experience-based analysis provides insights into when employees are more likely to leave the organization.
- Department-wise employee counts help HR understand workforce concentration across departments.
